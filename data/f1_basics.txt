Formula One (F1) is the highest class of international single-seater auto racing sanctioned by the Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile (FIA). The championship began in 1950 and consists of a series of races called Grands Prix held worldwide on purpose-built circuits and public roads.

Each race weekend typically includes three practice sessions, a qualifying session that determines starting order, and the Grand Prix race on Sunday.

Points are awarded to the top ten finishers in each race. The scoring system is:
1st: 25 points
2nd: 18 points
3rd: 15 points
4th: 12 points
5th: 10 points
6th: 8 points
7th: 6 points
8th: 4 points
9th: 2 points
10th: 1 point

An additional point is awarded for the fastest lap if the driver finishes in the top 10.

Two championships are awarded each season:

Drivers' Championship

Constructors' Championship
