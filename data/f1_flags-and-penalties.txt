Formula One uses a flag system to communicate with drivers:

Green Flag:
Track clear and racing allowed.

Yellow Flag:
Danger ahead, overtaking prohibited.

Double Yellow Flag:
Serious hazard, drivers must slow significantly and be prepared to stop.

Red Flag:
Race or session stopped due to unsafe conditions.

Blue Flag:
A faster car is approaching to lap a slower car; driver must allow passing.

Black Flag:
Driver is disqualified and must return to pits immediately.

White Flag:
Slow vehicle ahead.

Chequered Flag:
End of session or race.

Common penalties:
Time Penalty:
5 or 10 seconds added during pit stop or post-race.

Drive-Through Penalty:
Driver must drive through pit lane at speed limit.

Stop-and-Go Penalty:
Driver stops in pit box for 10 seconds without work being done.

Grid Penalty:
Driver starts further back at next race start.
