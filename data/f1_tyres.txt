Pirelli supplies tyres to all Formula One teams.

Dry tyres (slicks):
C1: hardest compound
C2
C3
C4
C5: softest compound

For each race, three compounds are selected:
Hard (white)
Medium (yellow)
Soft (red)

Wet tyres:
Intermediate (green) for damp track
Full Wet (blue) for heavy rain

Teams must use at least two dry compounds in a dry race unless weather conditions require wet tyres.
