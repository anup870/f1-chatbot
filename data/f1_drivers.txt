Famous Formula One drivers in history and the modern era:

Michael Schumacher:
7-time World Champion (1994, 1995, 2000–2004) with Benetton and Ferrari.

Lewis Hamilton:
7-time World Champion (2008, 2014, 2015, 2017–2020), holds records for most pole positions and race wins.

Ayrton Senna:
3-time World Champion known for exceptional qualifying speed and wet-weather driving skill.

Sebastian Vettel:
4 consecutive championships (2010–2013) with Red Bull Racing.

Juan Manuel Fangio:
5-time champion in the 1950s, one of the highest win-rate drivers in F1 history.

Modern Era Drivers:

Max Verstappen:
Multiple World Champion with Red Bull Racing, known for aggressive racecraft and consistency.

Charles Leclerc:
Ferrari driver known for qualifying speed and strong race pace.

Lando Norris:
McLaren driver recognized for smooth driving style and consistent performances.

Oscar Piastri:
McLaren driver and former junior champion, considered one of the top young talents.

George Russell:
Mercedes driver known for precision driving and race management.

Andrea Kimi Antonelli:
Mercedes young driver promoted from junior categories as part of a new generation of talent.

Fernando Alonso:
Two-time World Champion and one of the longest-serving drivers in Formula One history.

Pierre Gasly:
Race winner and Alpine driver known for strong midfield performances.

Esteban Ocon:
Alpine driver and Grand Prix winner with consistent race pace.

Alexander Albon:
Williams team leader and experienced midfield competitor.

Yuki Tsunoda:
RB team driver known for aggressive overtaking and qualifying pace.

Daniel Ricciardo:
Multiple Grand Prix winner famous for late braking overtakes.

Valtteri Bottas:
Experienced race winner and consistent qualifier.

Zhou Guanyu:
First Chinese Formula One driver competing in the modern era.

Kevin Magnussen:
Haas driver known for defensive racing style.

Nico Hulkenberg:
Highly experienced driver with strong qualifying performances.

Lance Stroll:
Aston Martin driver with podium finishes in mixed conditions.
