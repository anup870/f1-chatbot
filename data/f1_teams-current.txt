Formula One teams design and build their own cars but must follow strict FIA regulations. Each team runs two drivers.

Current Formula One Grid (2026 Season):

Red Bull Racing:
Drivers: Max Verstappen, Sergio Perez
Known for aerodynamic efficiency and strong race pace.

Mercedes-AMG Petronas Formula One Team:
Drivers: George Russell, Andrea Kimi Antonelli
Dominant during the hybrid era (2014–2020) and rebuilding with a new generation driver lineup.

Scuderia Ferrari:
Drivers: Charles Leclerc, Lewis Hamilton
The oldest team in Formula One history and one of the most successful.

McLaren:
Drivers: Lando Norris, Oscar Piastri
A historic team that returned to championship contention in the mid-2020s.

Aston Martin:
Drivers: Fernando Alonso, Lance Stroll
Focused on long-term development with strong technical investment.

Alpine:
Drivers: Pierre Gasly, Esteban Ocon
French works team competing for midfield and podium finishes.

Williams Racing:
Drivers: Alexander Albon, Logan Sargeant
One of the most historic teams in F1, rebuilding competitively.

RB (Racing Bulls / formerly AlphaTauri):
Drivers: Yuki Tsunoda, Daniel Ricciardo
Red Bull sister team used for driver development and talent progression.

Stake F1 Team Kick Sauber:
Drivers: Valtteri Bottas, Zhou Guanyu
Transitioning toward becoming the Audi works team in future seasons.

Haas F1 Team:
Drivers: Kevin Magnussen, Nico Hulkenberg
American-owned team focusing on efficient operations and steady points finishes.
