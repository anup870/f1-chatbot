Formula One race weekends have two formats: Standard and Sprint Weekend.

Standard Weekend Format:
Friday:

Practice 1 (FP1)

Practice 2 (FP2)

Saturday:

Practice 3 (FP3)

Qualifying (Q1, Q2, Q3)

Sunday:

Grand Prix Race

Sprint Weekend Format:
Friday:

Practice 1

Sprint Qualifying (Shootout qualifying)

Saturday:

Sprint Race (about 100 km, no mandatory pit stop)

Sunday:

Grand Prix Race

Sprint race points:
1st: 8
2nd: 7
3rd: 6
4th: 5
5th: 4
6th: 3
7th: 2
8th: 1

Qualifying sessions:
Q1: all drivers, slowest 5 eliminated
Q2: remaining 15 drivers, slowest 5 eliminated
Q3: final 10 compete for pole position
