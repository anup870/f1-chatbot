Formula One technical regulations define car design limits.

Minimum Weight:
Cars must meet a minimum weight including driver and fluids.

Fuel Flow Limit:
Fuel flow rate is restricted to improve efficiency.

Aerodynamics:
Front and rear wings are regulated to balance performance and overtaking.

Ground Effect Era (2022+):
Cars generate downforce primarily from the floor using venturi tunnels to improve racing and reduce dirty air.

Cost Cap:
Teams have a yearly spending limit to maintain competitive balance.

Parc Fermé:
After qualifying, teams cannot make major setup changes except under strict conditions.
